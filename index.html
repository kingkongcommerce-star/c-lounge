The Lounge

A premium cocktail lounge for slow, good nights

C Lounge is a two-level cocktail lounge at 176 Ngô Gia Tự in Thủ Dầu Một, Bình Dương — built around a floor-to-ceiling wall of spirits, a vintage chandelier, and warm string lights strung across every ceiling. It's a place to settle into a velvet armchair, order something carefully made, and stay a while.

Downstairs centers on the bar and its curated bottle wall; upstairs opens onto a quieter balcony lounge. Every corner is styled with intention — from the neon signage at the door to the sunset mural tucked down the hallway — for a genuinely photogenic, date-night or group-night kind of space.
